Good Samaritan Tips
  • Don't open wallets or go through personal items
  • Report immediately — the owner may be looking right now
  • If valuable (phone, ID), hand it to security office
  • Take a clear photo before posting
  • Mention exactly where you found it
  • Be available on the contact you provide
