Tips for a Better Report
  • Be specific with item name — include brand & model
  • Mention unique identifiers like serial numbers or engravings
  • Upload a clear photo to boost chances of recovery
  • Provide a specific location (building, room, floor)
  • Add a reward to motivate people to return your item
  • Mark as urgent if the item is critical (e.g. wallet, keys)
